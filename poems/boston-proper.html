Boston Proper

by Michael Penta

← All writingBoston · Poem

Hey kid! Where ya bin? Where’d ya get that aahh? You too good forrus now? Let me hear ya say caahh.

Wanna go to tha packie? Tha one next to Stahh? ’Member stealin’ those carriages An' headin' to tha bahh?

You ain’t a townie Ya sound so weeyid Talkin’ like ya from Cali Ya accent’s disappeeyid---

“Oh look at me, I’ve traveled hella faR”

“It’s wicked late. Let’s get in the caR”
