# Revision notes

## Among Sequoia Sempervirens — poem #1

- Original layout: narrow, vertical, read top-to-bottom.
- Experimental layout: page text in reverse vertical order, intended for a
  bottom-to-top reading corresponding to looking upward through a tree.
- Animated interpretation: reveal lines from bottom to top to clarify the
  intended direction without requiring readers to guess.

## Scroll-controlled animation

- Initial animation: a timer made lines appear above the page bottom, requiring
  viewers to scroll down before seeing the start.
- Revised animation: the descent is explicitly part of the reading.
  Viewers scroll down to the roots, then upward to uncover lines.
- Removed jumping text: a soft left-to-right ink mask reveals fixed lines.
- Previously revealed words remain visible; **Start over** resets the reading.
